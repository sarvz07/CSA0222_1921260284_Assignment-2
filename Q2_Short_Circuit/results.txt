Short-circuit principle:
- `a && b` only evaluates `b` if `a` is true.
- `a || b` only evaluates `b` if `a` is false.
- `a & b` and `a | b` always evaluate both sides.

This is why audit logging or verification functions should not be placed in a condition that can be short-circuited away.
